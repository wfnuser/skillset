# Format Presets

Read only the preset matching the requested time scale. User instructions and an established series format override these defaults.

## Weekly

Purpose: preserve the week's important inputs, movement, and lived texture while the events are still fresh.

Useful default structure:

```markdown
## 输入
## 研究
## 工作
## 生活
```

- Use a few descriptive `###` subheadings and ordinary paragraphs.
- Keep courses and papers to the strongest impression or insight.
- Do not force every research idea into its own subsection.
- Prefer one to three short paragraphs per subtopic.
- Add `输出` only when publishing work is central to that week.
- A recurring series may use front matter such as `type: weekly`, `period`, `series`, `issue`, and `status`.

A PhD Weekly title can follow an established pattern such as:

```markdown
# PhD Weekly #N

> 关于作者在 `YYYY-MM-DD` - `YYYY-MM-DD` 期间的人生积分 #N｜PhD Weekly #N
```

Treat this as a series preset, not a universal title. Preserve the author's pen name, institution suffix, numbering, and exact wording when known.

## Monthly

Purpose: discover what changed across the month and retain a few scenes that give those changes weight.

- Do not concatenate weekly entries.
- Identify one to three recurring themes or movements.
- A narrative title is usually better than “某月月报”.
- Chronology can remain the spine when the month contains a journey or transition.
- Use thematic sections when several parallel threads developed across the month.
- Include fewer, stronger images placed at their semantic anchors.
- The ending may remain unresolved; do not manufacture a monthly lesson.

## Annual

Purpose: reveal long arcs that were difficult to see while living through individual weeks or months.

- Start from evidence: calendar, photos, writing, projects, relationships, travel, health, and unfinished work.
- Group the year by turning points or persistent threads, not necessarily by twelve months.
- Distinguish accomplishments from changes in values, habits, confidence, or responsibility.
- Include failures and abandoned directions when they explain later choices.
- End with current orientation or open questions, not compulsory resolutions.

## Milestone or transition

Purpose: make sense of one boundary in life.

Possible spine:

```text
before -> decision or rupture -> movement and encounters -> arrival -> what remains unsettled
```

- Reconstruct the scene and sequence before interpreting it.
- Let people, place, and material details carry emotional weight.
- Avoid rewriting the event as inevitable in hindsight.
- Use dates and images to preserve provenance without turning the piece into a timeline dump.

## Open period

Purpose: summarize a user-defined interval or life stage that does not fit a calendar preset.

- Ask what boundary makes the period meaningful only when it cannot be inferred.
- For a multi-month period, prefer two to four cross-cutting threads or phases rather than week-by-week replay.
- Choose chronology for a journey and themes for parallel life domains.
- Use `milestone` only when one transition clearly organizes the surrounding material; otherwise keep `open-period`.
- Borrow metadata and title conventions from the nearest established series, but do not force weekly or annual headings onto it.
